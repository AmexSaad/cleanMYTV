# Bundled azan recordings

Prayer Time night-mosque background: AI-generated illustration, selected and approved as design number 2 by the project owner, with text removed for dynamic rendering. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). It is a decorative illustration, not a verified photograph of a particular mosque/location. The image does not change the licences of the recordings below.

Both recordings are freely redistributable under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The app displays these credits under **Sumber & kredit audio**. Neither author endorses cleanMYTV.

* **Regular azan:** “33937 ejaz215 call-to-prayer-from-the-prophet-s-mo”, by **ejaz215**, recorded at the Prophet's Mosque, Madinah. [Original source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:33937_ejaz215_call-to-prayer-from-the-prophet-s-mo.ogg). Original OGG file, unchanged.
* **Fajr / Subuh azan:** “Eid al-Fitr Fajr azan at Malmö Mosque – 19 August 2012”, by **Islamic Center Malmö**, muezzin **Besim Azemi**. [Original source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eid_al-Fitr_Fajr_azan_at_Malm%C3%B6_Mosque_-_19_August_2012.webm). Audio extracted from the original video and transcoded to OGG Vorbis; no phrases inserted or removed. The Fajr-specific recording is selected only for Subuh.

Prayer timetable and location catalogue: [Solat.my API](https://solat.my/api), which provides e-Solat JAKIM data and permits free personal and commercial use. Location catalogue retrieved 1 November 2026. No calculated AlAdhan fallback is substituted for zone schedules.
